Teaching

University courses, computational labs and student supervision.

Teaching approach

I have taught university mathematics since 2017 in Cameroon, France and Canada, in English and French. My teaching combines clear explanations, guided problem solving and computational activities. I connect mathematical concepts with applications and use exercises to encourage students to work independently.
