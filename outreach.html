Outreach

Mathematics education, inclusion and community engagement.

Science communication and outreach

My outreach work connects mathematics with everyday life, encourages young people to explore science, and promotes inclusion in STEM through educational projects and public engagement.

Selected outreach initiatives

2026

Math in the City

Founder and project lead of a bilingual mathematics outreach programme at Collège Louis-Riel in Winnipeg. Developed guided projects based on everyday quantitative problems and mentored five student teams, with support from PIMS, Acfas and the University of Manitoba.

2026

Tioh Academy Scholarship Programme

Founder of the programme, with 40 student recipients in its inaugural year.

2025–2026

MATh.en.JEANS
