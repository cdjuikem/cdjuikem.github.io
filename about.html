About

Academic background, research, teaching and engagement.

Profile

I am an applied mathematician working at the intersection of mathematical biology, dynamical systems and scientific computing. My research focuses on understanding biological mechanisms and designing mathematical approaches to questions in health, agriculture and the environment. I teach in English and French and am committed to making mathematics accessible through education and science communication.
