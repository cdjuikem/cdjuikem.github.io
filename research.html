Research

Mathematical and computational approaches to living systems.

Research overview

My research develops mathematical and computational methods to understand disease dynamics and biological responses, with a particular focus on finite-time dynamics. I combine mathematical analysis, numerical simulation and data analysis to investigate biological systems across scales.

Dynamical systemsMathematical biologyScientific computingData analysis

Current research

2025–present · University of Manitoba

Cellular mechanobiology and focal adhesion dynamics

With Professor Stéphanie Portet, I develop nonlinear ordinary differential equation models to investigate how intermediate filaments regulate actomyosin activity and focal adhesion dynamics. We study equilibrium stability, reactivity, transient amplification and relaxation, combining experimental fluorescence data with parameter estimation using genetic algorithms, sensitivity analysis and machine learning methods to classify dynamical responses across models and treatment conditions.

From within-host infection to population-level epidemiology

We developed a virtual cohort framework combining within-host ODE models and age-of-infection PDEs to investigate how individual variation affects population-level epidemic predictions.

Previous research

2023–2025 · University of Manitoba

Pathogen introduction and establishment in aquatic populations

With Professor Julien Arino, I studied deterministic and stochastic models of pathogen introduction, establishment and transmission across fish species under environmental stress and climate change. Methods include ODEs, continuous-time Markov chains and branching processes.

2019–2023 · Université Côte d’Azur and Inria

Modelling and control of perennial plant phytopathogens

My doctoral work at the French National Institute for Research in Digital Science and Technology (Inria), supervised by Frédéric Grognard and Suzanne Touzeau, investigated coffee leaf rust using stage-structured and impulsive models, stability analysis, Floquet theory and optimal control.

Methods and research interests

  • Deterministic and stochastic dynamical systems; ODEs, PDEs, impulsive and hybrid models.
  • Stability, bifurcation, transient dynamics and optimal control.
  • Numerical analysis, parameter estimation, sensitivity analysis and uncertainty quantification.
  • Scientific computing, data analysis and machine learning for biological systems.
